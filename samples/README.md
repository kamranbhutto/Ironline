# Sample files for testing uploads

| File | Type | Shows as |
|---|---|---|
| `assembly-plate-and-brackets.stp` | STEP AP214 assembly (public CAX-IF test model, from the occt-import-js test set) | 3D, with design colours |
| `cube-10mm.stp`, `cube-10mm.igs` | STEP and IGES, 10 mm cube (occt-import-js test set) | 3D |
| `l-bracket.stl`, `l-bracket.obj` | L-bracket 80 × 50 × 45 mm, generated for Ironline | 3D |
| `cover-flat-pattern.dxf` | Flat pattern 120 × 80 mm with 4 holes and a notch, generated for Ironline | 2D with size and cut length |

Upload them on a part (Parts & materials → open a part → Drawings and 3D models) or on a job (Files & 3D tab).
